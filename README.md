VICE NOIR — Midnight Cover Lab: an offline browser app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art. A midnight neon-noir direction: ink-black background, hot magenta + cyan duotone, diagonal comic-strip panels tilted off-axis, halftone dots, grain and vignette, a neon-glow Anton title with a Pacifico script subtitle. Four layouts (Diagonal Cut, Comic Strip, Hero + Duo, Full Bleed), four grades (Neon Noir, Vice Violet, Midnight Teal, Mono Ink), three photo slots with per-panel drag-to-pan, wheel-zoom and sliders, panel photo assignment, and caption, subtitle, tag and heat-star editing. Exports PNG covers at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Runs fully offline in the browser — no network requests, your photos stay on your machine. No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork or official fonts; Anton and Pacifico are vendored under the SIL Open Font License.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
